# gentcore

gentcore is a small, friendly social site for sharing what's on your mind and talking things through with other members.

**Live site:** https://mitchcross895.github.io/gents-socials/

## What you can do

- **Posts:** share short updates, with an optional photo. Like posts, leave comments, and like other people's comments.
- **Forum:** start threads in categories and reply to keep the conversation going.
- **Profiles:** set your name, handle, bio, profile picture and banner. Tap anyone's name or picture to see their profile and what they've posted.
- **Light and dark mode:** switch whenever you like, and gentcore remembers your choice.
- **Works everywhere:** made for phones, tablets and desktops alike.

Anyone can browse. Create a free account to post, comment, like and join the forum.

## Open source

gentcore is happily open source. Suggestions, issues and pull requests are welcome.
